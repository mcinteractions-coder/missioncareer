# Architecture rules
- Scope temporary public festival tokens to a route-aware shared wrapper, with decorative content in a dedicated component; this includes floating panels while leaving admin tools unchanged.
- Keep the static university logo catalogue as CDN asset pointers with original source URLs; avoid runtime logo services and preserve trademark artwork unchanged.
- Keep sourced study-cost figures (each with its source link or an explicit estimate marker) in src/lib/study-cost-data.ts and the budget formula in src/lib/study-budget.ts, both used by the comparison and calculator pages, with tests next to them; why: both tools must show the same, traceable numbers.
- Keep education loan EMI maths and lender rate presets (each with its official source link) in src/lib/education-loan.ts with tests next to it; why: rates change and the loan page must stay traceable.
- Keep the country quiz questions, country ratings and scoring in src/lib/country-quiz.ts with tests next to it, taking yearly costs from study-budget; why: quiz costs must match the calculators and the ratings are counselling judgements that will be tuned.
- Keep the University Finder catalogue as one compact data file per country in src/data/universities/ (row format and course codes in types.ts), decoded and searched in src/lib/universities.ts with tests next to it; why: the list is large and each country must be easy to review and update yearly.
